# Climate Change

**Summary**: Notes on climate impacts, extreme events such as floods, and the datasets used to study them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): An open global [[Data]]set (CC BY 4.0, Feb 2026) of 2.6 million historical flood events from more than 150 countries, extracted from news articles. It is distributed as a single 667 MB Parquet file. The authors are from Google and several universities. *Keywords: flood events, news-derived dataset, global flood database, Parquet, flood risk*
