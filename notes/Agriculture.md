# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on crop and field mapping, agricultural monitoring, and related tools and datasets.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): An open-source [[Python]] toolkit (Apache-2.0) that maps agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery and satellite [[Embeddings]], including Landsat, Sentinel-2, NAIP, Google Satellite Embedding and TESSERA. It offers seven engines (Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, DINOv3, Prithvi and others), optional SAM 2 refinement, and exports to GeoParquet or GeoPackage. *Keywords: field boundary delineation, agricultural parcels, satellite embeddings, geospatial foundation models, Python package*
